Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting our contact form or using our interactive tools:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, and message content when submitting an inquiry.
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymized server logs, IP addresses (used solely for edge rate-limiting and security filtering), browser user-agent, and timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Respond to your direct support inquiries or project quotes.
  • Detect, prevent, and mitigate malicious bot traffic and automated brute-force attacks.
  • Maintain system reliability and measure edge server response times.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed solely through our secure edge infrastructure providers (Cloudflare Pages).

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use invasive tracking cookies or third-party marketing pixels. We use standard browser localStorage strictly to store your interface theme preference (Dark or Light mode) and your client-side application demo state.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of any personal data you have transmitted to us. To exercise your rights, please contact our privacy team at support@launchflow-demo.com.
